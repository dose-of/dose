Studio

A nomad studio. Each city gives a new dose.

It started with a few months in Jaipur. I went to learn jewellery making and gemology, at the bench with the stone cutters and silversmiths of the old city. I stayed for the people.

DOSE is what came out of it: objects made by hand, in the place they come from, with the people who know how. The first collection is silver and stone. The next may be cloth, or something else. The city decides.

Every collection gets a number. No. 01 is Jaipur. No. 02 is on its way.
